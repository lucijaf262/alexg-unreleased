about the band

the
skin
cells

the skin cells were an american “pop rock that accidentally ended up being punk” band formed in havertown, pennsylvania around 2008.

the group consisted of alex giannascoli on guitar and vocals, samuel acchione on drums, and his brother colin acchione (aldo fisk) on bass guitar.
